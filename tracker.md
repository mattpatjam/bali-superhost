### 2026-10-14
- **Business plan started.** Bosses assigned Matthieu to build the brokerage business plan for the company, usable in Bali first, then Jakarta with Kozystay, then other countries. Scope locked: two linked documents (board-facing plan + operating manual on one shared financial model), Bali only for now, 30/60/90 plan within 1-2 weeks, full plan within 4 weeks.
- **Found that ~60% of the strategy already exists** inside `bsh-tuesday-meeting-draft.md` Sections 1-10. Not reusable as-is: written July 2026 pre-signing, stale title, mixes personal comp/ESOP into company strategy, and argues for hiring Matthieu rather than funding a business unit. Missing entirely: financial model, market sizing, unit economics, risk register, replication model, brand name.
- **Re-sequenced the plan.** Matthieu raised the model question (classical generalist vs luxury vs OLC-style exclusive projects) and asked to find the market gap first. Correct call, so positioning moved from Phase B to Step 1. Economics moved ahead of channels and headcount.
- **Market research done, 2026 Bali data.** Median sold $299K, leasehold asking median $300K / freehold $430K across 16,000+ listings. 2BR is the most traded segment at 32.8%. Canggu corridor 33.5% of transactions, Uluwatu-Nusa Dua 21.8%, Mengwi 17.9%, three corridors = 75% of pipeline. 11,751 villas for sale. Buyers: Australians 29%, Jakarta Indonesians 21%, Europeans 20%. 7.05M arrivals 2025, demand is not the problem.
- **The gap: credible numbers, not inventory or leads.** Three independent sources confirm the market has bifurcated into quality vs generic and that buyers were burned by projections. Exotiq names "buying on projected ROI rather than verified structure" as the number one buyer mistake. The July strategy doc had BSH's audited operating data listed as a *secondary point, worth a mention but not the lead*. That is backwards: it is the only element of the positioning no competitor can replicate.
- **Matthieu then disclosed what the 538-unit book actually is, and it damaged the first version of the thesis.** 80% Ubud, all one developer (Magnitude), all the same design, 1BR to 5-6BR, all under $1M, ~60% average occupancy. Consequences: Ubud sits outside the three corridors carrying 75% of transactions; the dataset is one product in one secondary area rather than market-representative; Channel 1 is a niche rather than the growth engine the July plan assumes; and Magnitude already carries quality-perception baggage with real clients, which is why Frans leaned toward a separate brand.
- **Reframed from portfolio to underwriting, which survives the concentration problem.** The asset is not "data on 538 villas", it is "we are the operator, so we know the real cost stack and realistic occupancy on any villa, including ones we do not manage". Quote net rather than gross, include the lines nobody shows (management fee, OTA commission, maintenance reserve, staffing, tax), and because BSH takes over management after the sale the projection becomes a commitment. Use the 60% as the weapon: everyone else inflates, BSH verifies. Works on externally sourced listings, which is what Matthieu confirmed he will actually be selling.
- **Geography fork identified.** Inventory says Ubud, market says Canggu and Uluwatu. Recommended sequencing rather than choosing: own Ubud months 1-4 (near-monopoly on Magnitude resales, fast cash, track record), then enter the corridors from month 4 on the underwriting position, funded by Ubud revenue. Ubud is the beachhead, not the business.
- Models scored: classical generalist rejected (crowded, no moat, needs 12+ agents against the 3-4 closer plan), luxury-only rejected (the 538 book is mid-market STR stock so the owner base does not feed it), OLC-style developer exclusives kept as Channel 2 not the core model (Legion already runs it at scale, slow start against a 90-day clock, off-plan is the distrusted product).
- Output: `Strategy/market-analysis-and-positioning.md`. PT PMA / freehold advisory positioning parked at Matthieu's direction despite the Feb 2026 rule change creating a gap.

### 2026-10-11
- Researched the brokerage CRM. Output: `Systems/crm-evaluation.md`.
- Requirements gathered from Matthieu: keep HubSpot for management and run a brokerage CRM alongside with two-way sync on contacts and properties (managed villas get listed for sale, management clients become brokerage clients), buy not build (dev team is for glue, Bali Exception's in-house CRM is the counter-example), under 10 users and under $500/mo, live for day one, website and brand do not exist yet so the site gets built against the chosen CRM's API.
- **Two findings that did most of the elimination work.** First, the US real estate CRM category does not transfer: Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail, Lofty, Sierra, BoomTown, Top Producer and the rest are built on MLS and IDX feeds, which Indonesia does not have. Second, HubSpot's custom objects are Enterprise only (confirmed on HubSpot's own KB, still true April 2026), so a villa has no object to live in without roughly $1,200/mo at 8 seats plus onboarding. That is the argument for management, and it is a cost argument rather than a taste one.
- Attio ruled out on the same mechanism: custom objects are Pro only at $79/seat annual, about $632/mo at 8 seats. Good product, genuinely best-in-class MCP server, simply over budget for the object model needed. Would be viable at 4 seats.
- Finalists scored one per category as requested. Zoho CRM Professional and Apimo CRM AI+ **tied at 4.16**, GoHighLevel scored 2.90 (no property object, no commission tracking, poor data export, prepaid wallet halts service when negative).
- Tiebreak reasoning, worth keeping: Apimo wins the property model outright (purpose-built for international non-MLS markets, native matching, multi-currency, commission tracking, own website product, €139/mo flat at any seat count). Zoho wins the integration surface outright (official WhatsApp Cloud API included, official MCP server with a published Claude setup guide, native Slack and Google Drive, full REST API). Recommended Zoho because configuration work has a deadline and missing integration surface does not: Apimo had no HubSpot connector, no MCP, and unverified Slack and WhatsApp support, which is custom integration work against a smaller vendor's API for every stated requirement.
- **WhatsApp answer to the "each agent uses their own number" question:** Meta now has an official feature called Coexistence that runs the Business app and the Cloud API on the same number simultaneously with real-time mirroring and no loss of chat history. Rolling out gradually, eligibility depends on country, BSP and the number, so it needs verifying for Indonesian numbers before promising it to agents. Unofficial QR-bridge tools are the real ban risk and are ruled out.
- Closio Estate noted and set aside rather than scored: its ICP (Jakarta agencies, 5 to 50 agents) fits BSH's shape, but Matthieu has a commercial relationship with the product and that project's own disqualifier list excludes Bali and Lombok for competitive protection. Flagged for the conflict, raise separately if worth revisiting.
